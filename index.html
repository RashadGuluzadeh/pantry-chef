    Your numbers

    ℹ️ Based on general guidelines: about 33 ml per kg of body weight, plus extra for activity and warm weather. Calories use the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. If you have kidney or heart conditions or follow a special diet, check with your doctor.

    ⚠️ What happens if you don't drink enough water?

    Your body is about 60% water. Losing even 1–2% of it — less than a litre — is already enough to feel the effects.

    🧠
    Headaches & poor focus

    The brain is very sensitive to water loss: memory, attention and mood get worse.

    😴
    Tiredness & low energy

    Thicker blood makes the heart work harder, so you feel weak and sleepy.

    💫
    Dizziness

    Blood pressure can drop, especially when standing up quickly.

    🫘
    Kidney stones & infections

    Too little water concentrates the urine and raises the risk of kidney stones and urinary infections.

    🧴
    Dry skin & lips

    Skin loses elasticity and looks dull and tired.

    🍽️
    Constipation & false hunger

    Digestion slows down, and thirst is often mistaken for hunger.

    🌡️
    Overheating

    In hot weather or during sport, dehydration can lead to heat exhaustion or heat stroke.

    🚨
    Warning signs

    Dark yellow urine, dry mouth, thirst, headache, rapid heartbeat. Pale yellow urine means you're doing well.
